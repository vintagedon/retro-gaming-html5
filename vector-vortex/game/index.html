Vector Vortex

Clear the Crawler wave on a 24-lane wireframe tube.

How to Play

Ride the rim of the 24-lane tube. Crawlers advance outward from the far end; a Crawler that reaches your rim costs a life, and so does a Crawler shot that crosses your lane. Three hits end the run.

Left Arrow or A moves left around the rim. Right Arrow or D moves right, wrapping at the seam. Hold Space to fire inward along your lane; shots have a short cooldown and six may be active at once.

Escape or P pauses. A kill scores 100. Clear the wave when every Crawler has spawned and been resolved.
