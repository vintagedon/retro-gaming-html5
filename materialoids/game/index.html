MATERIALOIDS

Wireframe Asteroids

You pilot a wireframe vessel through a field of irregular rocks. Thrust, rotate, fire. The void has no friction. Hit a rock, it splits. Hit the smallest rocks, they vanish. Clear the wave. Repeat. As you go deeper, hostile contacts arrive — UFOs, snipers, spinners, hunters. Each fires its own random bullet patterns. None are friendly.

How To Play

Rotate
← / → · A / D · gamepad d-pad LR or left stick
Thrust
↑ / W · gamepad up / A · tap and hold THRUST
Fire
Space · gamepad RT or X · FIRE button
Hyperspace
Shift / H · gamepad LT · panic button — random teleport, brief invulnerability, but every use risks instant death (1-in-12 chance).
Pause
P / Esc · gamepad Start
Inertia
The void has no friction. Velocity persists until you thrust against it. Plan your moves.
Wraparound
Edges of the screen wrap. Drift off the right and you reappear on the left.
Asteroids
3 sizes. Large splits into 2 medium. Medium splits into 2 small. Small just dies. Each split is a multi-rock chain — clear the chain.
UFO
Wave 1+. Drifts across, jukes vertically, fires aimed and spread patterns. 800 pts.
Sniper
Wave 3+. Glows brighter as it charges, then fires fast precise shots. Tighter accuracy. 1,200 pts.
Spinner
Wave 5+. Rotates and emits radial bullet patterns — stars, crosses, and spirals. No aiming, but bullets go everywhere. 1,500 pts.
Hunter
Wave 8+. Chases you down. Fires rapid twin and cone shots. Don't let it get close. 1,800 pts.
Extra ship
Earned at every 10,000 points. Maximum 9 in reserve.
